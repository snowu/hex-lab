Observability & incident stories

What to measure, and how to narrate an incident like a senior.

The three pillars, pragmatically

  • Metrics: RED for services (Rate, Errors, Duration), USE for resources (Utilisation, Saturation, Errors). Alert on symptoms (SLO burn rate), not causes (CPU).
  • Logs: structured, with a correlation/trace id. Never log secrets, tokens, or full account numbers / PII.
  • Traces: show which dependency made the request slow. Propagate context across queues too.

SLO example: “99.9% of balance requests succeed in < 2s over 30 days” → error budget = 43 minutes of badness/month. Spending the budget deliberately (risky rollouts) is a feature, burning it silently is an alert.

Narrating an incident (use this order)

  1. Detect: how did we find out (alert vs customer)?
  2. Mitigate first: rollback, feature flag, fail over. Diagnose second.
  3. Diagnose with data: what you ruled out and why.
  4. Fix & verify.
  5. Learn: blameless review, the systemic fix, a follow-up that actually shipped.

Template: incident story

“On [date] our [service] started returning [symptom] for [N]% of [users]. We detected it via [alert/customer] after [X] min. I [role], we rolled back / flagged off within [Y] min. Root cause was [cause], which we proved by [evidence]. The systemic fix was [change to design/process/test], and I owned [follow-up] which reduced [metric] by [number].”
